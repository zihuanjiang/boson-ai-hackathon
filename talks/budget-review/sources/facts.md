# Spring campaign fact sheet

## Budget

- The approved Q1 campaign budget is $30,000. Dana (finance lead) approved it on 10 February.
- Spending above the approved budget needs a new approval from Dana before it is committed.

## Campaign

- The spring campaign starts on 1 March and runs for six weeks, to 12 April.
- Eli (marketing) runs the campaign day to day.
- Eli can pause the campaign at any time; a pause is reported to Dana the same day.
- The campaign targets existing customers in Canada only; there are no paid placements outside Canada.

## Data

- Customer data collected by the campaign is kept for 90 days after the campaign ends and then deleted.
- Fay's team owns the deletion job and confirms the deletion to Dana in writing.
- Weekly spend and sign-up numbers go to Dana every Monday.
