# What comes next

Planned work beyond the hackathon (doc/brief_updated.md "Nice-to-haves"; README "Limits").

- [NEXT-8] The next steps and future plans after the hackathon: in-person meetings, other platforms like Google Meet and Teams, more languages, a faster and calibrated check, and a team's own documents.
- [NEXT-1] In-person meetings: echo cancellation for one shared room microphone, and telling speakers apart from a single microphone.
- [NEXT-2] Other meeting platforms such as Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, through the same participant approach as the Zoom bot.
- [NEXT-3] More languages: Higgs Realtime can answer in the language of the question, so a multilingual meeting is a natural next step.
- [NEXT-4] A faster check: running a smaller Claude model first, or checking only lines that look like proposals or confirmations, to bring Alice's question closer to the confirming line.
- [NEXT-5] Calibrating the check on real meetings, so its confidence matches how often it is right, and letting a team set how often Alice may speak up.
- [NEXT-6] Uploading a team's own documents and decision records instead of a prepared package, and exporting the notes to the team's tools.
- [NEXT-7] An optional Higgs Avatar so Alice has a face in the call, once the voice flow is solid.
