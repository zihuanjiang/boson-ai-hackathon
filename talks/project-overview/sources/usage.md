# Using it in a meeting

How people work with Alice in a Zoom call: everything happens by voice, with no keyboard (README "Run it").

- [USE-1] To start, someone hands Alice the floor by voice, for example "Alice, take it away", and she begins the presentation.
- [USE-2] To ask a question during the presentation, just talk over her: she stops within about a second, looks the answer up in the documents, shows the source on screen, answers, and picks up where she was cut.
- [USE-3] In the discussion Alice stays quiet. To ask her something, say her name: "Alice, …". For a short while after she answers, a follow-up question or correction needs no name.
- [USE-4] Anyone can ask Alice to speak faster or slower ("Alice, speak a bit faster", "slow down", "maximum speed"); the new speed holds for the rest of the meeting.
- [USE-5] To end the discussion, someone says "let's wrap up": Alice sums up the decisions with their owners first, then the open questions.
- [USE-6] The side panel shows the evidence behind every answer and question, the live transcript, and the meeting notes: confirmed decisions with owners, clarifications and open questions.
- [USE-7] Preparing a meeting means writing its package: the slides, the agenda, the documents Alice may cite, and a script Claude writes for her; a validator checks the package before the meeting.
- [USE-8] Alice works best when people speak one at a time and stay muted when they are not talking: a cough or a remark while she presents counts as an interruption.
