# Online meetings

How the facilitator takes part in an online meeting (doc/architecture.md "In Zoom"; README "Limits").

- [ZOOM-1] The facilitator joins a Zoom meeting as its own participant, Facilitator (AI), and shares the meeting page as its screen.
- [ZOOM-2] To interrupt the facilitator or get its attention in Zoom, people just speak up: anyone who talks while it speaks cuts it off by voice.
- [ZOOM-3] In Zoom the facilitator hears the meeting's voices only; it never sees raised hands or the chat.
- [ZOOM-4] Speaker names in Zoom are read best effort from Zoom's web page (its active-speaker display), never from the audio, so a line can carry the wrong name or none; this was not yet verified live on 24 October 2026.
- [ZOOM-5] For a repeatable demo the participants can be prerecorded clips voiced by Higgs TTS 3, played for the room and streamed to Higgs Realtime in real time with the microphone off; the page labels them as recorded.
- [ZOOM-6] On 24 October 2026 the facilitator joined a real Zoom meeting as its own participant, shared its slides as its screen, and presented the script Claude wrote for it word for word.
