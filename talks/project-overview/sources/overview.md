# On the Same Page: what it is

The project in four statements (CLAUDE.md, doc/brief_updated.md).

- [WHAT-1] On the Same Page is a real-time AI meeting facilitator: it presents a short deck live, answers interruptions from cited documents, listens to the discussion and keeps the decisions in meeting notes.
- [WHAT-2] It speaks up only when a decision is about to contradict the documents or an earlier clarification; the goal is to stop apparent agreement from becoming a mistaken decision, not to replace human judgement.
- [WHAT-3] It was built in two days at the Higgs Audio Hackathon 2026 of Boson AI and UofT MScAC in Toronto, on 23 and 24 October 2026.
- [WHAT-4] Higgs Audio is central: Higgs Realtime (higgs-realtime-1.5-preview, the newest model) presents, listens and speaks; Higgs speech to text (higgs-stt-3.1) transcribes inside the Realtime session; Higgs TTS 3 voiced the prerecorded participants of the earlier test runs.
