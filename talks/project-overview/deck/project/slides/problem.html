The meeting after the slides

Everyone nods.

Nobody opened
the documents.

Build 1: Because the hard part comes after the slides. A tired room nods along. Everyone agrees. Build 2: And the plan they agree on contradicts the documents nobody opened. So the meeting ends in agreement, and nobody is on the same page. Delivery: lower and slower on the first line, two short dry sentences on “A tired room nods along. Everyone agrees.” Steady, with weight on “contradicts”; the last line quiet, like a punchline (a callback to the name), then a pause.
