Higgs Audio Hackathon 2026 · Toronto

Imagine…

Two days. One demo. A very tired room.

Imagine you’re an engineer, up since yesterday morning, building a hackathon project for Boson AI. Feature after feature, bug after bug, one more take of the demo. And in an hour, you present it, to a room as tired as you are. Are you frustrated? Nervous? Dreading the one question you can’t answer? Then let me take it from here. Delivery: these are the first words of the presentation, with no greeting before them. About twenty-five seconds, a lively storyteller at a brisk pace: warm at the start, quicker through the list with a light stress on each item, a brief pause after “as tired as you are” and after each question, then a lift on the last line.
