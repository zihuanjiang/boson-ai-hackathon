Build 1: Here’s the Zoom workflow. I join your Zoom call as my own participant, share these slides, and present Claude’s script. Next, you’ll test the discussion check, and we’ll keep the confirmed decision and its owner in the notes. Build 2: Now, my limits. I hear one mixed stream, so overlapping voices can blur together. Names come from Zoom’s speaker display, so I can mislabel who said what. And Claude’s check takes a few seconds, and it can miss one. Delivery: about twenty-eight seconds over the two builds, word for word; confident and brisk on the first, plain with no apology on the second, a brief pause after “Now, my limits.” The final demo uses Ken and Partner speaking live in the Zoom call.
