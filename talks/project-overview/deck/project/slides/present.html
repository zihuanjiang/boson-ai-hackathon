Interrupt: it looks up, cites, resumes

Build 1: Here’s how. One Higgs Realtime session runs the whole meeting, one talking point at a time, from a script Claude wrote beforehand. Build 2: Interrupt me, and I stop mid-sentence. I look the answer up, show you the source, and pick up where I was cut. In the demo, Ken cuts in about a second and a half into “Interrupt me”: “Sorry, quick question: in Zoom, can people raise a hand to interrupt you?” The facilitator stops at once, looks it up, and answers in a sentence or two from the sources: in Zoom it hears voices only, not raised hands or the chat, so people just speak up. The answer is kept as the meeting’s first clarification. Then it picks this point up with the script’s prepared line, never from the top: As I was saying, I stop, look it up, show you the source, and pick up right where I left off. Just like that. Claude wrote the script before the meeting; nothing Claude does is on the live path while the facilitator presents. Everything is live: there are no recordings; if Higgs Realtime doesn’t answer, the presentation pauses and the host tries again.
