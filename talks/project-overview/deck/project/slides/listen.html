When the slides end, I listen. Higgs transcribes every line, and Claude checks each one for a conflict with the documents, a hidden disagreement, or a detail nobody settled. Before you confirm a plan like that, I ask one neutral question. Delivery: about seventeen seconds, word for word. Calm and clear; count the three kinds off evenly; weight on “before you confirm”. What happens behind it: the application checks every citation and quote Claude reports against its own records, and lets the facilitator ask only when the group is confirming and the evidence holds; otherwise it stays silent or shows a silent suggestion. A decision goes into the notes only once the participants confirm it, and its owner, when one is named, must be one of them.
