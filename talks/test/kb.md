# Facts for Q&A (test talk)

- This is the test talk for Relay, a voice co-presenter, used to check the pipeline end to end.
- The narration is rendered ahead of time with Higgs TTS 3, with a timestamp for every word.
- Live replies come from Higgs Realtime, in the same preset voice as the narration.
- When someone cuts in, Relay stops on the word, answers live, then resumes from the first word of that sentence.
- Ken holds the floor control: a tap on the space bar stops Relay and opens the microphone for one turn.
