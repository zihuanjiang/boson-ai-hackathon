## 1. The idea (one paragraph)

Presentation skills are important, where many software engineers find themselves hard to express what they did in a clear and concise way to represent to the audience. The evolution of coding agents and text-agents can, in theory, help them build slides and write script but in-deep cannot truly solve the issue of nervous, and the requirement of presentation. We observe this obstacle and notice that modern llms are expert in representing dense and professional work with metaphors that non professionals can understand, yet we would like to adopt this into the agent capabilities. We would like to build a framework that can automatically 'present and engage' within the meeting, and the real human contributor will then be the backup on any directional change, and clarification on high-level ideas, with where AI can also guide the conversation.
